Privacy policy

AFTER 0.6.0 offers free games with ads and optional one-time purchases. Spin the Bottle and A Kiss for Two require a pack or promo code. Complete AFTER includes all currently available games and removes ads. All categories remain free.

Effective: November 24, 2026
2026-11-24-commerce-1

1. Controller and scope

Controller: Fabián Del Castillo, Ecuador. Privacy and support contact: fabijuegos10@gmail.com.

This policy covers AFTER 0.6.0 for Android, with Google AdMob and Google Play purchases. You do not create an AFTER account. Purchasing, restoring and requesting ads require internet. Games are local; we have no game server or our own usage analytics.

2. Data stored on the device

Names or nicknames are optional and identify participants. They are stored locally until you remove them from Players or clear AFTER’s data.

The language and preferences for sound, vibration, reduced motion and onboarding are saved to remember your settings.

When you accept the terms, the latest acceptance is stored: document version and hashes, UTC date according to the device, app version, language and confirmation that you are at least 16. The record does not store your birth date, identity or player names.

Google Play processes payments and links purchases and redeemed codes to your Google account. We store the signed receipt, product ID and purchase token on the device to validate and restore access. We do not receive your full card details or send receipts to our own server. Previous simulated purchases do not grant access.

3. During games

Charades processes the phone’s tilt. Finger Picker uses touch positions; it does not read fingerprints or identify people.

Roles, votes, words and game positions stay in memory, without a remote history. Random selections are for entertainment.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location or contacts. It uses internet for ads, privacy and Google Play Billing. It does not request the Android advertising ID.

4. Retention and deletion

You can delete names in Players and acceptance in Settings > Privacy > Delete local record. To delete all data, use Android Settings > Apps > AFTER > Storage > Clear data.

Local data stays until you delete or replace it. The app uses the system’s private storage and disables its Android backup. Manufacturer transfer tools may follow different rules.

5. Support and website

If you email support, we receive your address, message and attachments to handle your request, meet applicable obligations and resolve issues. Do not send passwords or unnecessary identity documents.

Requests are retained while being handled and, generally, for up to 90 days after resolution. A legal obligation or claim may require specific information to be kept longer.

Email and the document website on GitHub Pages are external services. Their providers may process technical data, such as a website visitor’s IP address, outside Ecuador. This does not give them the names, roles or games stored in AFTER.

Provider policies: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement and https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Your rights and changes

You may request information, access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, suspension or portability where applicable. Contact fabijuegos10@gmail.com. The controller cannot remotely read or delete data that exists only on your device.

You may complain to Ecuador’s Superintendence of Personal Data Protection or the competent authority in your country. AFTER is for people aged 16 and over; a legal representative’s permission must be obtained when required.

You will be informed before significant changes in data use. Reading this policy or accepting the terms does not consent to advertising or new optional uses.

7. Advertising and privacy choices

Google AdMob may collect and share your IP address (to estimate general location), technical app or device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic data, including crashes and performance. These are used to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and maintain the service. We do not send player names, roles or game words to Google.

We request non-personalized ads with teen treatment and a maximum PG ad content rating. Non-personalized does not mean no data is processed. Google may use local storage and identifiers other than the advertising ID to provide the service.

Google UMP presents your regional privacy choices. Change them in Settings > Ad preferences when available. Accepting terms does not replace consent. Declining ads does not block free or purchased games. Complete AFTER disables ad requests once its purchase is validated.

Consent is used where required by applicable law; necessary security and fraud prevention operations may have other legal bases explained by Google. Data may be processed outside your country. Google encrypts transmission and applies its retention periods and transfer mechanisms. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
